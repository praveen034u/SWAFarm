# Two-unit POC acceptance plan - NOT RUN

Record POC001 and POC002 separately: board revision/hash, serial, operator/date, instrument IDs, firmware commit/hash, measurements, photographs and pass/fail. No physical tests were performed during CAD preparation. Stop and diagnose any failure before progressing; retain results with this package.

1. **Incoming inspection:** reconcile parts and orientation with BOM/CPL; examine bridges, polarity, connector numbering, thermal-pad process evidence and unplated mounting holes. Check enclosure/lead clearance. With all supplies removed, check for rail shorts and unintended GND/ISO_GND/EF_RTN connections. Semiconductor paths can affect meter readings; do not infer insulation ratings from a continuity beep.
2. **First power:** disconnect valves, external sensors, bus and auxiliary loads. Use a current-limited 12 V bench supply; start conservatively, increasing limit only after ruling out shorts (wireless startup can cause brownout at low limits). Measure 12V_SW, 5 V, 3.3 V and isolated 5 V against module tolerances and IC operating limits; record startup/inrush and steady current. Investigate any cycling or heating. Never bond EF_RTN to grounded scope clips; use suitable differential/isolated measurement.
3. **Power characterization:** progressively load the actual intended sensor/auxiliary/relay loads; keep normal total input <=1.2 A and characterize the nominal 1.489 A eFuse limit. Verify UVLO/OVP thresholds, startup, transient response, capacitor margin and thermal rise using appropriate lab equipment. Reverse polarity, surge and fault-energy tests require a controlled qualified setup, not ad-hoc field testing. Confirm fuse/TVS coordination; no IEC surge qualification is inferred.
4. **Firmware safety gate before valves:** implement OLATA=0 before IODIRA=0 on MCP23S17, default-one concurrency, staggered starts, bounded watering timeout, heartbeat/watchdog and communication-loss handling. Verify all eight outputs off at cold boot and deliberate commanded shutdown. Test MCU reset, brownout, hang and communications loss. **MCP outputs may remain energized across MCU software reset; there is no independent hardware fail-safe.** If safe shutdown cannot be guaranteed, restrict to supervised bench use with manual power/water shutoff. A welded relay is not detected or independently interrupted.
5. **Relay mapping:** use a meter and low-voltage dummy loads first; verify K1-K8 correspond to software channels 1-8 and COM/NO/NC labels. Test each, then eight together while recording supply current and driver/relay temperatures. Integral driver clamps protect relay coils, not remote valve wiring.
6. **USB/debug:** externally power the board; check no unintended VBUS backfeed. Verify programming and at least 100 reconnect/enumeration cycles in both USB-C orientations. Main USB geometry targets 90 ohms, but short branches/via asymmetry require measured functional validation. Capture boot/reset behavior and firmware logs.
7. **Radio:** confirm exact module variant and UART2 AT communications, antenna connection and India-region configuration before transmitting. Measure conducted/link behavior with compliant settings. A module approval does not establish final product or global-region certification.
8. **RS485:** verify isolated rail and both connectors/polarity, TX/RX/direction, loopback and communication under load. Board has hardwired 120-ohm termination and 680-ohm bias; use as a bus endpoint with only the intended two end terminations. Do not deploy multiple such terminated nodes indiscriminately. Functional isolation is not mains safety isolation.
9. **Sensors:** begin with all mode shunts open, calibrated low-energy voltage source and no external live wiring. Confirm six channel mappings and divider response at 0, 2.5, 5 and 10 V; nominal ADC node is Vin x 100/332 (3.012 V at 10 V), before tolerances/ADC calibration. Netlist-verified mode table for each channel: voltage = JP_VBYP open, JP_IGND open; current = both closed. JP_VBYP bypasses 232k series resistor, JP_IGND grounds the 150-ohm burden; the 100k bottom resistor remains in parallel. Nominal 4/12/20 mA node voltages are 0.599/1.797/2.996 V. Verify actual shunt continuity with power removed before current tests. Never apply 10 V in current mode, use mixed/unknown modes, or hot-switch shunts. Verify MCU input limits and calibrate all six channels.
10. **Valve and endurance tests:** use separately isolated, fused 24 VAC supply, first one valve then staged eight-valve worst case. Measure inrush, holding current, contact/connector/trace temperatures and interference with radio/USB/sensors. Validate suppression with the selected valve/harness; test supply interruption and manual shutoff. Repeat enclosed worst-ambient operation with agreed temperature limits and sufficient run duration. Record leakage/pressure behavior with qualified plumbing support.

Acceptance for supervised POC requires both units to pass applicable tests and all deviations to be signed off. Unattended use, industrial ambient claims, ingress/ESD/EMC/surge compliance and global sale require additional engineering/qualification. This plan does not assert those requirements are met.
